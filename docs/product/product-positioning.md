# Product Positioning

Fenéla is a small accountability app for moments when everything feels too much.

It helps a user move from overwhelm to one small action, with optional AI support and optional reminders.

## Core positioning

```text
Fenéla helps you choose one small action when everything feels too much.
```

This is the clearest explanation of the product.

## Product loop

```text
overwhelm -> one small action -> gentle accountability -> daily return
```

This loop is the product. Every feature should support it directly.

## Audience

Fenéla may be useful for people who:

- feel mentally overloaded;
- struggle to start when tasks feel too large;
- need a small daily point of focus;
- want gentle accountability without a complex planning system;
- prefer simple tools over dashboards and productivity frameworks.

Fenéla is not limited to one audience, but it should remain useful for people who need low-friction support.

## Problem

When someone is overwhelmed, the problem is often not a lack of ambition.

The problem is that everything feels too large at once.

A full planner can make that worse. A dashboard can add more decisions. A habit system can add pressure. Fenéla keeps the scope smaller: one small action for today.

## Product claim

Fenéla does not promise transformation.

It supports a smaller next step.

That is enough for the MVP.

## What makes Fenéla different

Fenéla is different because it does not try to become a large productivity system.

It stays focused on:

- one action;
- low pressure;
- optional support;
- simple return;
- calm language;
- clear boundaries.

Fenéla is intentionally limited in scope.

## What Fenéla is not

Fenéla is not a therapy app, medical tool, productivity platform or coaching program. The full boundary list is in the [README](../../README.md) and [MVP scope](mvp-scope.md).

This should stay clear in all public documentation.

## AI positioning

AI is optional support.

AI can help the user:

- make an action smaller;
- find a practical starting point;
- rephrase an anchor;
- avoid over-planning.

AI should not be positioned as the main value of Fenéla.

Correct positioning:

```text
Optional AI assistance helps suggest smaller anchors.
```

Avoid:

```text
AI-powered life coach.
```

```text
Your personal AI therapist.
```

```text
A full AI productivity planner.
```

## Reminder positioning

Reminders are optional accountability.

They should feel like support, not pressure.

Correct positioning:

```text
You can set a gentle reminder, or continue without one.
```

Avoid:

```text
Never miss your goals again.
```

```text
Stay accountable every day or lose your streak.
```

The tone should stay calm.

## Public repository positioning

Fenéla should be positioned as:

```text
A public MIT-licensed accountability app and portfolio project.
```

Use:

- public repository;
- public MIT-licensed project;
- publicly available application;
- MIT-licensed software;
- portfolio project.

Avoid calling Fenéla an open-source community project unless that positioning is deliberately changed later.

## Portfolio positioning

Fenéla shows:

- product thinking;
- scope control;
- calm UX decisions;
- optional AI integration;
- reminder infrastructure;
- public documentation;
- maintainable implementation;
- clear MVP boundaries.

It should not exaggerate.

This is a small working product with documented product, UX and architecture decisions.

## Positioning risks

Main risks:

- making the app sound clinical;
- making it sound bigger than it is;
- over-positioning AI;
- turning reminders into pressure;
- adding productivity language that increases cognitive load;
- calling it open-source in a way that implies community governance.

## Decision

Fenéla should be positioned as a small, calm accountability app.

Reason:
That is the clearest match with the actual product and MVP.

Trade-off:
The app may sound less ambitious than larger productivity or AI products.

Impact:
The product becomes easier to explain, safer to publish and more credible as portfolio work.

## Summary

Fenéla should not try to sound bigger than it is.

It should stay focused on one small action, optional support and a simple daily return loop.
